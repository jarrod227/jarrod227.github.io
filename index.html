ABOUT

About me

My background is in electrical and computer engineering. My interests live where human signals meet physical systems.

I earned my B.S. in ECE with a mathematics minor at Rutgers, and I’m now pursuing an M.S. in Biomedical Engineering on the research track at CMU, with graduation expected in May 2028.

Alongside research, I contribute motor-control firmware to CMU Robotics Club’s Laika quadruped, connecting my interests in embedded systems and robotics.

Outside the lab, I enjoy cooking, playing soccer, and playing guitar. Japanese curry and katsudon are two of my favorite dishes.
