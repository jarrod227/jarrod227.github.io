THE IDEA

From neural activity to language.

I built a PyTorch pipeline for intracortical speech decoding on the Brain-to-Text ’24 benchmark. A bidirectional GRU encoder feeds a diphone CTC decoder, with skip-diphone and smoothness-loss variants evaluated through a five-variant ablation study and Kaldi WFST decoding.

MY CONTRIBUTION

What I worked on

01

Sequence modeling

Implemented the GRU encoder and diphone CTC pipeline, then explored skip-diphone and smoothness objectives.

02

Controlled comparisons

Ran five ablation variants to examine phoneme-level changes against the baseline.

03

Word-level diagnosis

Compared phoneme and word error rates and found that the phoneme-level gain did not carry over to word error after WFST decoding.

ENGINEERING CHALLENGE

Check whether phoneme gains survive decoding

The best variant reduced phoneme error rate from 20.48% to 19.75%, but this gain did not translate to lower word error. The baseline retained the best reported word error rate, 18.07%, with a 5-gram language model. The result made the decoding interface as important to examine as the neural model itself.
