THE IDEA

Closer to the signal.

In the Neuromechatronics Lab at Carnegie Mellon University, advised by Douglas Weber, I am designing a 32-channel high-density EMG rigid-flex PCB. The design combines an 8 × 4 electrode array, a 500 mm flex lead, and an Omnetics connector.

MY CONTRIBUTION

What I worked on

01

Electrode interface design

Developed the rigid-flex PCB in KiCad around the array, flex lead, connector, and channel mapping requirements.

02

Design automation

Used Python to automate component and layout generation, making repeated geometry and channel assignments easier to manage.

03

Connectivity and review

Verified channel connectivity in the design files and prepared preliminary manufacturing files for review.

ENGINEERING CHALLENGE

Keep geometry and channel identity consistent

For an electrode array, spatial arrangement and electrical mapping must agree. My work combines layout automation with connectivity checks so the design can be reviewed as a recording interface, with a clear mapping from electrode locations to connector channels.
