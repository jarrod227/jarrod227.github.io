THE IDEA

Closer to the signal.

In the Neuromechatronics Lab at Carnegie Mellon University, advised by Douglas Weber, I designed a 32-channel high-density EMG flex PCB with polyimide and FR-4 stiffeners. The design combines an 8 × 4 electrode array at 8 mm pitch, a 300 mm flex lead, and an Omnetics connector.

MY CONTRIBUTION

What I worked on

01

Electrode interface design

Developed the flex PCB in KiCad around the array, flex lead, connector, and channel mapping requirements.

02

Design automation

Used Python to automate component and layout generation, making repeated geometry and channel assignments easier to manage.

03

Verification and manufacturing

Verified channel connectivity in the design files, worked through the fabricator’s DFM/DFA review, prepared the assembly drawing, and approved the production Gerbers.

ENGINEERING CHALLENGE

Keep geometry and channel identity consistent

For an electrode array, spatial arrangement and electrical mapping must agree. My work combines layout automation with connectivity checks so the design can be reviewed as a recording interface, with a clear mapping from electrode locations to connector channels.

RESULTS & SCOPE

What the work shows

Array

32 channels in an 8 × 4 array at 8 mm pitch, with 4.22 mm electrode pads.

Interconnect

300 mm flex lead at 0.10 mm trace and space, ending in an Omnetics connector.

Design checks

Zero DRC and ERC violations in KiCad; fabricator DFM/DFA review completed and 16 units ordered.

Current boundaries

The boards are in fabrication and have not yet been tested. Fabrication yield, recording quality, and in-use performance are not claimed here. The illustration on this page is a conceptual array diagram, not a manufacturing drawing.
